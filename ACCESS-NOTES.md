# Access and Verification Notes

[简体中文](ACCESS-NOTES.zh-CN.md) · [Back to Main Index](README.md) · Verified on: 2026-09-16

"Entry page accessible" means the specific material or text-image page could be read; "Directory accessible" means the project introduction or directory could be read. Neither guarantees that original image files, interactive zooming, downloading, or video playback have been verified. The following notes only record observations within the research environment and do not represent the accessibility of these sites across all regions or networks.

## Historical Photographs of China

[Image Archive](https://hpcbristol.net/) · [Example: Hawker outside basket shop, Beijing](https://hpcbristol.net/visual/Hv28-20)

An entry to Chinese historical photography maintained by the University of Bristol. Useful for researching figures, shops, architecture, occupations, and street scenes. The example is from the Hedda Morrison collection, with the original site estimating the date between 1933–1946.

**Entry page accessible.** The example provides a link to the original image; download was not tested. The entry notes Harvard's copyright and that reproduction requires written permission. Hedda Morrison's related photographs overlap with Harvard's underlying collections, so they are linked here rather than counted as an independent resource. Previous visits experienced rate limits, but the entry was successfully opened this time.

## Virtual Shanghai

[Project Overview](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Presentation/Overview) · [Example: Astor House Hotel towards Suzhou Creek](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Photos/Images?ID=1)

Historical photographs, maps, and urban research materials for Shanghai. Useful for researching streets, architecture, transportation, and spatial relationships. The photo and map archives are included as a combined resource.

**Entry page accessible.** The example provides a "Get original resolution" option; download was not tested. There is a discrepancy between the date field and the architectural description in the example, so the exact year of photography requires further verification and is not stated as a confirmed year here.

## MIT Visualizing Cultures | John Thomson's China

[Theme Entry](https://visualizingcultures.mit.edu/john_thomson_china_03/index.html)

Showcases the four-volume *Illustrations of China and Its People* published in 1873–1874, sourced from the Beinecke Library at Yale University. Useful for references on late Qing figures, clothing, occupations, street scenes, and architecture. Note that the publication year is not the same as the year the photos were taken.

**Directory accessible.** Album and image entries are available; individual image zooming or downloading has not been verified.

## Harvard | Joseph F. Rock Collection

[Collection Overview and Online Access](https://library.harvard.edu/collections/joseph-f-rock-collection)

Historical photographs taken by Rock during his expeditions in Yunnan, Sichuan, Gansu, Tibet, and other regions. Useful for supplementing references on local environments, figures, and expedition photography in Southwest and Northwest China.

**Directory accessible.** The official introduction links to the online collection; individual items within HOLLIS and downloading were not further tested in this round.

## The Tibet Album

[Project Entry](https://tibet.prm.ox.ac.uk/)

A project by the Pitt Rivers Museum, University of Oxford, and the British Museum featuring photography of Tibet from 1920–1950. Materials can be searched by person, place, date, photographer, and album.

**Directory accessible.** The site provides maps, thematic searches, and instructions for zooming; individual images and interactive operations were not tested in this round.

## MIT | The Empress Dowager and Photography

[Illustrated Essay](https://visualizingcultures.mit.edu/empress_dowager/cx_essay02.html)

Photographs of Empress Dowager Cixi, detailing clothing, jewelry, seating, screens, and photographic settings. Useful for references on Qing court imagery and furnishings. The staged photographic settings should not be directly equated with everyday living spaces.

**Entry page accessible.** The text contains image citations and descriptions; original images and downloads were not tested.

## Wellcome | Peking Union Medical College Architectural Archives

[1922 Archives and Access](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/pf63heps)

Historical overview and architectural descriptions of the Peking Union Medical College from 1922, listing 29 digital images on the page. Useful for references on medical institutions and architectural history.

**Entry page accessible.** A viewer is available, but downloads were not tested. This specific archive is explicitly marked CC BY-NC 4.0, which cannot be extrapolated as a uniform license for the entire Wellcome collection.

## UW–Madison | East Asian Collection

[East Asian Collection](https://search.library.wisc.edu/digital/AEastAsian)

An entry to East Asian materials at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Can be used as a further search entry for Chinese historical photographs and postcards; the materials are not entirely about China and are not strictly limited to the 1930s.

**Directory accessible.** The old entry URL has been confirmed to redirect here; a specific link to Ma Zhanshan provided in the report failed to open this round, so it is not marked as a verified single photograph.

## North China Railway Archive

[Search Entry](https://codh.rois.ac.jp/north-china-railway/) · [Open Access Statement (Chinese)](https://codh.rois.ac.jp/north-china-railway/announcement/index.html.zh-cn)

A research database based on the photographic collection of North China transportation held by Kyoto University. Photographs of North China railways, cities, and environments along the lines can be searched by date, station, and tags.

**Directory accessible.** The current public entry is accessible and cannot be summarized as requiring an academic account. Original materials include wartime propaganda photography; some tags and descriptions on the site are AI-generated and should be distinguished from the original records when cited. Original image files and downloads were not tested.

## David Rumsey | Historical Maps of China

[Directory of China-related Maps and Images](https://www.davidrumsey.com/luna/servlet/view/all/where/China)

An entry to maps, nautical charts, atlases, and expedition publications filtered for China. Further filtering can be done by date, author, and type.

**Directory accessible.** Map titles and dates could be read in this round; original image downloading or zooming was not tested. Unverified dynamic individual links were not adopted.

## Toyo Bunko Rare Books Digital Archive

[Archive Entry](https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/index.html.en) · [Example: *Atlas von China* Vol. 1, p. 43](https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/Lc-39/V-1/page/0043.html.en)

Rare atlases, maps, and travel publications organized by NII Digital Silk Road and Toyo Bunko, where materials related to China and the Silk Road can be found.

**Entry page accessible.** The example provides high-resolution, IIIF, and PDF entries, though actual file downloads were not tested. For usage conditions, see the [original site's license explanation](https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/license/).

## Digital Maps of Beijing

[Theme Entry](https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/beijing-maps/concept/index.html.en)

Compares changes in the city of Beijing across different eras through maps and photographs, linking the Qianlong Capital Map, Beijing maps, and photographic atlases. Useful for finding spatial references for city walls, roads, and neighborhoods.

**Directory accessible.** Part of the Digital Silk Road platform with Toyo Bunko, but included as an independent thematic resource; map tiles and interactive comparisons were not tested in this round.

## MIT | Modern Sketch (Shidai Manhua)

[1934–1937 Image Directory](https://visualizingcultures.mit.edu/modern_sketch_03/index.html)

*Modern Sketch*, published in Shanghai. The page lists access to 39 issues from 1934–1937, linking to the Colgate University collection. Useful for references on publication layouts, cartoons, figure representation, and urban visual culture.

**Directory accessible.** Individual issues, the external collection links, and zoomed images were not verified one by one.

## MIT | Dianshizhai Pictorial Theme

[Theme Entry](https://visualizingcultures.mit.edu/dianshizhai/index.html)

Images and research guides for the *Dianshizhai Pictorial* in late Qing Shanghai. Useful for references on news illustrations, urban imagination, and print visuals. This entry is a curated theme, not a full database of the publication.

**Directory accessible.** Galleries and downloads were not verified individually. The illustrations should be distinguished from real-world photography.

## Chinese Posters

[Website](https://chineseposters.net/) · [Example: Model Women Workers Screen (2)](https://chineseposters.net/posters/e14-998)

Propaganda posters searchable by themes like figures, labor, and agriculture. The example is marked as early 1950s, recording the designer, publisher, and IISH collection number, supplementing visual materials after 1949.

**Entry page accessible.** Image citations and collection information are present, but downloads were not tested. Propaganda posters cannot replace documentary photographs of daily life.

## Taiwan Memory

[Search Entry](https://tm.ncl.edu.tw/index)

An entry to local history and digital archives in the Taiwan region, useful for further searching for old photographs, postcards, and other visual materials, supporting temporal and geographic searches.

**Directory accessible.** Individual photographs were not checked in this round; access and usage conditions should be checked according to the specific archives, and no judgment is made on reachability from other network environments.

## International Dunhuang Programme (IDP)

[Digital Collection](https://idp.bl.uk/collection/) · [Example: Dunhuang Star Atlas Or.8210/S.3326](https://idp.bl.uk/collection/7861395E5F814419BA05483EAB254832/)

A further search entry for Dunhuang and Silk Road visual manuscripts. The star atlas example is a paper-based diagram, supplementing scientific and manuscript visual materials prior to modern photography.

**Entry page accessible.** An IIIF entry is available, though the image itself was not tested. There is a discrepancy between the date field and the text description for the example, so the exact date is not determined in this index; the page notes that copyright information is being updated.


## Second Round: Adding Recent Materials (2026-09-16)

The following 10 items were selected from the second round of GPT and Gemini research, with the original site pages re-verified. The verification only represents this access attempt and does not guarantee accessibility across different network environments. Image pixel display, original image downloads, and actual video playback were not verified.

### Beijing Silvermine

[Project Introduction](https://www.beijingsilvermine.com/about) · [Curated Gallery](https://www.beijingsilvermine.com/gallery)

The project introduction and gallery pages are accessible. The date range comes from the overall collection description and does not mean each curated photo has an independent date. The gallery provides links to larger images, but the image files themselves and downloads were not tested; a unified open license was not confirmed.

### Wikimedia Commons | Exploring Images of China by Decade

[Category: China in the 2000s](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:China_in_the_2000s) · [Example: A student having lunch in Tieling High School, 2018](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_student_having_lunch_in_Tieling_High_School.jpg)

The category page and specific file pages are accessible. The example date is 2018-05-29, credited to Kychn, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Original image downloads were not tested, and this license is not generalized to the entire category.

### Xinhua News Agency | National Album

[Column and Episode Directory](https://www.xinhuanet.com/video/gjxc/index.htm)

The column text and episode directory are accessible; the column description states that the main photographs are from the China Photo Archive. Episodes were not verified individually, and video playback or extraction of original photographs was not tested. The 2016 broadcast start date does not equal the date the footage was captured.

### University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa | Photographs of China, 1987-1988

[Collection Directory](https://digital.library.manoa.hawaii.edu/collections/show/95) · [Example: Hard sleeper carriage, 1988](https://digital.library.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/show/53292)

The collection page and hard sleeper entry page are accessible. Both the example's title and date field point to 1988; the text describes two color photographs, and the rights field is CC-BY (version not specified). Image pixels, zooming, and downloading were not tested.

### Ian Teh | Undercurrents

[Photography Project and Captions](https://www.ianteh.com/undercurrents-1)

The project description and multiple photograph captions are accessible. The 1999–2008 range covers all five stories and cannot be directly applied as the specific year for each photograph. Image files and downloads were not tested, and individual usage licenses were not verified.

### ChinaFile | Living by the Rivers

[Living by the Rivers](https://www.chinafile.com/multimedia/depth-of-field/living-rivers)

The theme text and project introductions are accessible, published on 2019-02-25. The introduction to Shi Yangkun's series notes that shooting began in March 2018. Externally linked works and image downloads were not verified individually, and we do not assume all photos were taken in 2019.

### University of Hong Kong | Hong Kong Image Database

[Hong Kong Image Database](https://digitalrepository.lib.hku.hk/hkimage)

The official collection introduction page is accessible, and the 1840s to 1990s range comes from this page's description. Single photographs from the 1990s and image downloads were not verified in this round, so we do not promise an adequate volume of online photos for every decade based on this.

### Greg Girard | Shanghai and Hong Kong Photography

[Author Website and Photo Captions](https://www.greggirard.com/) · [Phantom Shanghai](https://www.greggirard.com/phantom-shanghai/)

The author's homepage is accessible, including photo captions for Shanghai in 2001, 2002, and 2005, and the 1974–1989 range for HK:PM. The Phantom Shanghai page is accessible and provides image links. Image files and downloads were not tested, and open licenses were not confirmed.

### Shanghai Street Stories

[Example: A Slice of Time on Moganshan Road](https://shanghaistreetstories.com/?p=8941)

The specific article is accessible, published on 2015-07-26; the text explicitly describes photos from circa 2009 and covers return visits in other years. We do not mark all photos in the full text as 2015. Image downloads and reuse licenses were not verified.

### Edward Burtynsky | China

[China Photography Series](https://www.edwardburtynsky.com/projects/photographs/china)

The author's project text is accessible, and thematic categories and large image links are visible. The currently retrieved page does not have complete year-by-year dates for each photo, so we do not adopt the unverified specific years from the report. Image files and downloads were not tested, and open licenses were not confirmed.
