# More Resources and Clues

[简体中文](MORE-RESOURCES.zh-CN.md) · [Back to Main Index](README.md) · Updated: 2026-09-16

The following 24 items are kept as unverified clues and are not counted among the 27 featured resources. The availability evaluations from the reports have not automatically been treated as actual verification results for this index. Subsequent checks will update the notes, and entries meeting the criteria will be moved to the main index.

"To be confirmed" does not mean "no photos," and is specifically divided into:

- **Dates or descriptions to be confirmed**: Photographs or artwork introductions exist, but individual dates, locations, or sources are missing. They can serve as references but are temporarily not used to prove coverage of a specific decade.
- **Access to be re-verified**: Clues for collections or specific entries exist, but the tool encountered authentication, 403 errors, or loading issues in this round; we cannot conclude the site is dead based on this, nor can we claim to have seen the photos.
- **Specific content to be located**: Currently, there are only main sites, collection introductions, or report clues; China-related photos, atlases, or videos still need to be found.
- **Usage conditions to be confirmed**: Materials can be found, but downloading, redistribution, or licensing is not yet clear. Unclear licensing itself does not prevent inclusion as a browsing entry; image display, original image downloading, and video playback are recorded separately.

The same resource may have several pending confirmations simultaneously. The featured list also allows for localized unknown information, which is noted in the corresponding entries and access records.
